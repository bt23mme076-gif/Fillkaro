FillKaroFill once. Apply everywhere.

Your details

Fill these once. FillKaro matches them to form questions like “Name of candidate”, “SSC %”, “Year of passing 12th” or “Active backlogs”. Everything saves automatically and stays in this browser.

Keep separate details for placements, internships or hackathons. The popup fills with the profile chosen here.

Saved answers

Answers to questions outside your details — “Why do you want to join?”, “T-shirt size”, “How did you hear about us?”. Add them here, or fill a form yourself and click Save my answers from this page in the popup.

Similar wordings match too.

Filling

When a form asks for a percentage you haven’t saved, FillKaro uses CGPA × this number (and percentage ÷ it for CGPA). Most colleges use 9.5; set 0 to turn off.

Keyboard shortcut: Alt+Shift+F. Change it at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Backup

Move your details to another browser or keep a copy. The file contains your personal details — keep it private.

Privacy

FillKaro stores everything in chrome.storage.local on this device. It has no server, sends nothing anywhere, and only touches a page when you click Fill, use the shortcut or the right-click menu. It never submits forms, never ticks consent boxes, and skips password, OTP, card, Aadhaar and PAN fields.
