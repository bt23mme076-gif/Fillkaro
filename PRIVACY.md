# FillKaro Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 October 2026_

FillKaro is a browser extension that fills web forms with details you save in it.

**What FillKaro stores.** The details and answers you type into FillKaro's settings, or choose to save from a form with "Save my answers from this page", plus your settings. This can include your name, contact details, address, date of birth, academic records and profile links.

**Where it is stored.** Only in your browser, using Chrome's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) on your device. FillKaro has no server and no account.

**What FillKaro sends.** Nothing. FillKaro does not transmit, sell, share or analyse your data. It contains no analytics or tracking.

**When FillKaro reads a page.** Only when you click Fill or Save, press the keyboard shortcut, or use its right-click menu, and only on the tab you are on. It writes your saved values into that page's form fields, or reads the values you typed there when you ask it to save them.

**What it never touches.** Password, OTP, captcha, payment card, bank account, IFSC, UPI, Aadhaar and PAN fields. It never submits forms, and never ticks a consent, terms or declaration checkbox (it only ticks options of multiple-choice questions that match an answer you saved).

**Your control.** Edit or delete any detail or answer in FillKaro's settings, export a backup, or use "Delete all my data". Uninstalling the extension also deletes its stored data.
